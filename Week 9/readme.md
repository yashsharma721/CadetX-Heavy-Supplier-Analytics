CadetX Heavy Supplier Analytics – Week 9



**Project Overview**

This repository contains my Week 9 work completed as part of the CadetX Virtual Work Experience Program. This week focused on developing an interactive Procurement \& Payment Analysis Dashboard using Microsoft Power BI to analyze supplier performance, purchase orders, invoices, and payment activities.



**Dashboard Features**

Total Purchase Value KPI

Total Suppliers KPI

Total Payments KPI

Top 10 Suppliers by Purchase Value

Payments by Method

Top 10 Purchase Orders by Value

Invoice Value vs Payment Amount
