CadetX Heavy Supplier Analytics – Week 7

This repository contains my Week 7 work completed as part of the CadetX Virtual Work Experience Program. This week focused on developing an interactive Sales Analysis Dashboard using Microsoft Power BI to analyze sales performance and generate meaningful business insights.
