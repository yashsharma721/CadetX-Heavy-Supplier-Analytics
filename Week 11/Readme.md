CadetX Heavy Supplier Analytics – Week 11



**Project Overview**

This repository contains my Week 11 work completed as part of the CadetX Virtual Work Experience Program. This week focused on developing an interactive Inventory \& Stock Movement Dashboard using Microsoft Power BI to analyze inventory value, stock movements, product performance, and supplier contribution.



**Dashboard Features**

Total Inventory Value KPI

Total Purchase Value KPI

Total Stock Movement Quantity KPI

Inventory Health KPI

Top 10 Products by Stock Movement

Stock Movement by Type

Top 10 Products by Inventory Value

Top 10 Products by Current Stock

Top 10 Suppliers by Purchase Value
