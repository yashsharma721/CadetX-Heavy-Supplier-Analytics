CadetX Heavy Supplier Analytics – Week 10



**Project Overview**

This repository contains my Week 10 work completed as part of the CadetX Virtual Work Experience Program. This week focused on developing an interactive Stock Movement \& Procurement Analysis Dashboard using Microsoft Power BI to analyze inventory movements, purchasing activity, and product-level procurement insights.



**Dashboard Features**

Total Stock Movements KPI

Total Purchase Line Value KPI

Total Purchase Quantity KPI

Total Purchase Orders KPI

Total Stock Balance KPI

Top 10 Products by Purchase Value

Stock Movement by Type

Stock Movement Trend
