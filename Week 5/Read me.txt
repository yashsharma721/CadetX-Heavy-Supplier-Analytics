# CadetX Heavy Supplier Analytics – Week 5

## Project Overview
This repository contains my Week 5 work completed as part of the CadetX Virtual Work Experience Program. This week focused on writing SQL JOIN queries to analyze supplier purchases, inventory, invoices, payments, and branch stock.

## Tasks Completed
- Top 10 Suppliers by Total Purchase Value
- Top 10 Purchase Orders by Purchase Amount
- Products Below Reorder Level
- Invoice and Payment Analysis
- Top 10 Branches by Total Stock
- Payment Method Analysis

## Objectives

- Generate business insights for procurement and inventory management.
